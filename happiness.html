The Lightness You Forgot You Had

Notice this: right now, nothing is required of you. Not one thing. Let your shoulders find out how far down they can actually go.

You are allowed to feel good for no reason. You don't need a permission slip, an achievement, a reason the size of your worry. Happiness doesn't have to be earned back like a debt — sometimes it just arrives, the way morning light lands on a wall without asking if the wall deserves it.

Think of the last time you laughed so suddenly it surprised you — a real one, the kind that comes up from somewhere below your ribs. Let that feeling come back now, uninvited, the way it did then.

You are, in this exact moment, further along than you were a year ago. Softer in the places that needed softening. Steadier in the places that needed steadying. That's worth something. That's worth this — a small, unearned pocket of good.

So here's what happiness actually asks of you: not gratitude, not proof, not a story about why. Just this — stay a little longer in the good part. Don't rush to explain it away.

You get to have this. Right now, you get to have this.
