To the shape they left behind,

I know you still set the table for one more sometimes, out of habit, before you remember. I know their voice still catches you off guard in ordinary places — a stranger's laugh, a song on the radio, a coat like the one they used to wear.

That's not you being unable to move on. That's just what love does when it loses its address — it doesn't leave, it just stops having anywhere to go, so it collects in the small unguarded moments instead.

Nobody can tell you how long this should take, and anyone who tries is wrong. Grief doesn't run on a schedule. It runs on how much the person mattered, and it sounds like they mattered enormously.

You're allowed to still be in this, however long "this" has been. You're allowed to laugh again without it meaning you've forgotten. You're allowed to fall apart again without it meaning you haven't healed. Both can be true in the same week, the same hour.

They were here. That's not erased by them being gone. The love doesn't stop being real just because it has nowhere left to land — it just becomes part of how you carry yourself now, quietly, always.

Take your time. There's no version of this you're doing wrong.
