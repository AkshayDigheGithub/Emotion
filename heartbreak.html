It isn't the missing. It's the rehearsing.

You've had the conversation again tonight — the one that never happens. You said the right thing this time. They understood. It still didn't help.

That's the part that exhausts you. Not the missing exactly, but the rehearsing. Your mind keeps staging it, looking for the version where it ends differently.

There isn't one. You've checked every angle by now, at every hour, and the ending holds.

Somewhere in the last few weeks you stopped missing the person and started missing the future — the one with them in it, already furnished, that you had quietly been living in.

That future was real to you. Losing it is a real loss, even though nobody sends flowers for it.

You will stop rehearsing. Not tonight, and not because you decided to. One ordinary evening you'll notice the conversation didn't run, and you'll already be somewhere else.

Until then: you're not being pathetic. You're being someone who meant it.
