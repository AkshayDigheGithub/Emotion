It's still dark, and the sky is already changing

You can't see it yet, not really — the light hasn't reached the part of the sky you're looking at. But somewhere past the horizon, it's already begun. That's how mornings work. The change starts before there's any proof of it.

You've been asking things to be different for a while now, and nothing you can point to has shifted. But things are almost never over at the exact moment they feel most over. Underneath the stillness, something is usually already moving.

You don't need to know how this resolves to keep going. You just need to be willing to still be here when it does. That's all hope has ever actually asked of anyone — not certainty, just a willingness to stay for the next part.

Think of every hardest chapter you've already survived. You didn't know how those ended either, while you were inside them. And yet here you are, on the other side of every single one, reading this.

That's not luck. That's evidence. You are someone things get better for, even when it takes longer than you wanted.

The dark isn't permanent. It never has been. Just keep facing the direction the light is coming from.
