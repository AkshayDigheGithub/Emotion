Nothing is asking anything of you right now

Let your eyes rest on this, not read it exactly, just move across it slowly.

Breathe in for four. Hold for four. Out for six. You don't need to count out loud — just let the rhythm happen underneath everything else you're thinking about.

Your shoulders have been climbing toward your ears for longer than you realized. Let them drop. Let your jaw unclench. Let your hands, wherever they are, go soft and open.

There is nowhere you need to be inside this moment. The list is still there, waiting, but it can wait a little longer — it isn't going anywhere, and neither is this quiet.

Picture still water. Not a metaphor for anything, just water, flat and unbothered, holding the sky's reflection without needing to do anything else with it.

You are allowed to be that still. You are allowed to just exist for a few breaths, without producing, without fixing, without becoming anything other than what you already are, sitting here, reading this.

Slow down even more. There's no version of this moment that's late.
