You keep almost sending it

The message is written. It has been for a while now — in your head if not in your phone — and you keep almost sending it.

You've found reasons not to. Good ones, mostly. It's late. It's been too long. It would reopen something you have nearly finished closing.

But the wanting doesn't take instruction. It arrives when a song does, or when something happens that only they would have found funny, and you reach for your phone before you remember you'd stopped doing that.

Missing someone isn't a problem to be solved. It's evidence — of a real thing, that was real, and hasn't finished being real in you yet.

Nobody gets to time that for you. Not them, not the people who think you should be over it, and not the version of you reading this and feeling faintly embarrassed.

You can hold someone in mind without holding them in your life. Those are different rooms.

Put the phone down tonight. You can miss them in the morning too.
