Dear you,

I'm not going to tell you it gets better. Not yet. Right now I just want to sit here with you, in the part where it's heavy, and not rush you toward the door marked "fine."

Some things don't resolve. They just get carried differently over time. That's not failure — that's what it looks like to love something, or someone, enough that its absence has weight.

You don't have to perform your sadness for anyone, and you don't have to hide it either. It's allowed to just exist in you, quiet, like weather passing through a house that was built to withstand it.

If you're crying right now, let it happen without narrating it. If you're past crying, into that flat grey stillness — that's sorrow too, just a different room in the same house.

There is nothing wrong with you for feeling this. There never was. Grief and sorrow are just love that ran out of somewhere to go, so it stayed in you instead.

Stay here as long as you need. I'm not going anywhere, and neither is what you lost — not really. It's just changed shape.

With you,
