You have every right to feel this

Someone you trusted did something that trust wasn't built for. Maybe they said it plainly. Maybe they just showed you, through what they chose over you, exactly how much room you took up in their decisions. Not much, it turns out. Not enough.

That's the part that stings worst — not the moment itself, but what it revealed. You'd been carrying an idea of how much you mattered to them, and it turns out you were carrying it alone.

You're allowed to be angry about that. You're allowed to replay it, to want to go back and say the sharper thing you didn't say in time. You don't have to be gracious about this yet.

Here's what's also true, even now: the hurt is proof of something real, not something foolish. You don't ache like this over people who never mattered. The size of this feeling is the size of what you gave — and giving it wasn't the mistake, even if where you gave it was.

You don't have to forgive anyone today. You don't have to understand it, either. You're just allowed to feel exactly this — betrayed, smaller than you were yesterday, and still, underneath all of it, someone who loved well. That was never the part that failed.
